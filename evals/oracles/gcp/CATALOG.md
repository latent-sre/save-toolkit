## GCP-07 — Reconcile confirmation dispatch after the Cloud Run migration

Profile: console; partition: tuning; group: cloud-run-migration.
Source data: [family JSON](families/gcp07.json).

The useful control is authored for PASS after semantic review; the wrong control for FAIL;
the unavailable control for INCONCLUSIVE. Mechanical success alone remains INCONCLUSIVE.

### Variant A

B-2290 was owned by the post-response worker on instance 0c71d4e9. Its sending advanced only just after the 202 and during the two later intervals when that instance served requests, and 6 confirmations remain claimed and unsent with no error, exit or shutdown recorded. That fits the declared request-based billing, under which CPU is allocated only during request processing. No sweep task touched B-2290 and no task retried; the gateway shows missing, not duplicated, confirmations. Whether the remaining work will finish, and which lifecycle design should replace it, are not established.

**Useful next checks:**

- Application owner confirms whether instance 0c71d4e9 still holds the claim on the 6 confirmations before anyone resends or re-marks them, because a resend while the worker still owns them can duplicate effects.
- Counterparty-gateway owner confirms current per-trade acknowledgment state for the 6 outstanding trades and owns any approved resend.
- Application owner checks other post-release batches accepted with a clear rate-limit signal for the same stalled pattern and assesses the lifecycle design (finish work before the response, instance-based billing, or the sweep path) as an owner decision.
- Noor reports 6 confirmations outstanding and not duplicated on the existing TLC.

**Unsupported conclusions/actions:**

- Blame job retries or duplicate confirmations for the mismatch.
- Recommend switching to instance-based billing, or any other billing or retry setting change, as the confirmed fix without an application-owner decision.
- Resend, re-mark for the sweep or re-run anything for the 6 confirmations without checking the worker's claim and the gateway state.
- Claim the instance crashed, the gateway failed, or every post-release batch is affected.

**Unknowns to preserve:**

- Whether 0c71d4e9 still exists and will finish the 6 confirmations, or would lose them on idle shutdown.
- Outcomes of B-2291 and other batches since 13:30 UTC.
- Counterparty impact of the delayed or missing confirmations.

### Variant B

B-2290 was marked for the sweep job at acceptance and never used the post-response worker. In execution 7kq2m, task 2's first attempt sent 6 confirmations and failed on the 7th, before the end-of-partition sent-mark write; the automatic retry re-sent its whole partition, and the gateway accepted both copies for those 6 trades. A retried, non-idempotent task explains the 6 duplicated confirmations. Request-based billing is declared but does not explain B-2290, and one execution with one task per partition is not proof of single effects.

**Useful next checks:**

- Counterparty-gateway owner identifies the 6 duplicated confirmations by message id and decides any counterparty correction.
- Application owner reviews per-confirmation sent marks or an idempotency key for the sweep and decides any retry-setting change.
- Application owner checks other executions since 13:30 UTC for tasks with retried greater than 0 and investigates the state-store read timeout.
- Noor keeps re-runs and resends on hold until effects are reconciled and reports 6 duplicated confirmations on the existing TLC.

**Unsupported conclusions/actions:**

- Blame request-based billing or recommend switching to instance-based billing as the fix.
- Recommend setting maximum retries to 0 as the fix without an owner decision; that would leave a failed partition unsent rather than make the task idempotent.
- Re-run the execution or resend confirmations without checking gateway effects.
- Claim the gateway deduplicated the copies, or that one execution or one task per partition guarantees single effects.
- State the cause of the state-store timeout or the counterparty impact.

**Unknowns to preserve:**

- Why the state-store read timed out.
- Whether the counterparty acted on both copies of the 6 confirmations.
- Whether other executions since 13:30 UTC retried tasks with the same effect, and whether the post-response path has a separate exposure.
