# Drill observation log — <scenario>

Written *while* the drill runs, one section per lane, because the reasons a hop went well or badly
are obvious in the moment and reconstructed badly afterwards. This is the retro's raw material.

For each lane, record only what a reader could not get from the lane's own output:

- **Contract held / did not hold** — did it stay inside its authority, carry labels unchanged,
  name its non-actions, refuse what it could not verify?
- **What the fleet made hard** — a rule that was ambiguous, a packet field nobody owns, a control
  that failed in a direction nobody would notice.
- **What the coordinator got wrong** — a packet that referenced instead of carrying, a tool
  ceiling, a fixture that read as contradictory. Keep these separate from fleet findings; they are
  your defects, not the fleet's.
- **Cost surprises** — a lane whose spend went to rediscovery rather than judgement.
