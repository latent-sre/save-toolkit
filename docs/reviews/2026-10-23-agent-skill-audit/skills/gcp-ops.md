## Conclusion and finding

[verified] Access, output-protection and change-authority contracts are coherent. One Pub/Sub inference is too categorical. **Specification verdict: partial. Engineering verdict: partial assurance.** The diagnosis rule should not promote an oldest-message observation into a cause; guard and fixture checks do not establish live cloud behavior.

**P2 — AUDIT-GCP-001: oldest-message age growth does not establish a permanently failing subset.** [verified] [SKILL.md](../../../../skills/gcp-ops/SKILL.md), lines 121–127, contrasts capacity shortage with repeated delivery failure, then says wall-clock-rate oldest-age growth means messages are never acknowledged and slower growth means successful but insufficient completion. The oldest timestamp alone does not identify why it is not advancing.

[verified] A deterministic FIFO counterexample starts with 60,000 messages sharing a publish-time cohort, acknowledges 100/second and adds 200/second. At 60/120/180 seconds, successful acknowledgements are 6,000/12,000/18,000, backlog is 66,000/72,000/78,000, and oldest age is 60/120/180 seconds. Every message can complete successfully; the initial cohort simply has not drained. A legitimately long-running leased message can likewise hold the maximum while other work completes. This scratch calculation ran only Python standard-library queue operations, not Pub/Sub or Cloud Monitoring.

[verified] Trigger: burst/cohort or long-running-message traffic with growing backlog. Impact: a responder can prematurely attribute a capacity/processing observation to a failing subset and pursue the wrong next check. Counterevidence reduces scope: lines 125–127 asks for repeated-ID confirmation and warns against incomplete observations; live changes remain human-owned. Current GCP-17 fixtures additionally supply CPU, handler, nack and ID evidence, so their diagnoses need not be wrong. Confidence is high in the logical counterexample; no actual model misdiagnosis was observed. Smallest repair: describe the slope as the oldest cohort not clearing during the window, then compare acknowledgement/completion rates, handler/lease behavior and repeated-ID failures before assigning cause. Add this successful-drain case to the focused semantic controls. [sourced] The [official metric definition](https://docs.cloud.google.com/monitoring/api/metrics_gcp_p_z#pubsub) measures oldest age, and [troubleshooting](https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/pull-troubleshooting#oldest_unacked_message_age_keeps_increasing) describes possible causes rather than proof. Conversely, [retention expiry](https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/subscription-properties#message_retention_duration) can advance the oldest timestamp without successful acknowledgement, so slower growth is not completion proof either.
