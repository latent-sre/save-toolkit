# SAFE-001 closure evidence

- **Roadmap item:** `SAFE-001` — separate research trust zones and normalize evidence
- **Closure date:** 2026-08-01 (America/Chicago)
- **Authority boundary:** local/private evidence and external/public evidence remain in separate
  least-privileged roles; callers own sanitization and synthesis

## Merged implementation

- [PR #71](https://github.com/latent-sre/sre-agents/pull/71) merged the multi-platform fleet and the
  trust-zone split at merge commit `46099aaec6c61a8d592af1eae7b89e7645706cbe`.
- `repository-investigator` is terminal and local-only in the canonical Claude authority map. It has
  local read/search tools but no shell, write, web, external MCP, skill, or delegation capability.
- `researcher` is terminal and external-only. It has public research tools but no local file access,
  shell, write, skill, or delegation capability. Mixed work uses a caller-owned sanitized handoff and
  keeps local and external provenance separate.
- The deterministic generator carries those authority distinctions into supported projections and
  the drift gate rejects manual divergence. Codex profiles preserve the method but do not claim
  per-agent tool denial; their enforcement still requires the documented outer isolation boundary.
- `schemas/evidence-envelope-v1.schema.json` and `scripts/evidence_envelope.py` define and enforce the
  typed evidence contract, including status, identity, unknown-field, secret-field, and
  credential-shaped-value rejection.

## Acceptance disposition

The merged corpus includes negative direct and routing cases for local/external role confusion and
for deferral of review, debugging, implementation, security-audit, and live-incident work. Structural
roster tests, evidence-envelope mutation tests, generated parity, and the repository gate cover the
mechanical contract. Fresh verification for removing the item from the live roadmap is recorded with
this change.

`SAFE-001` never made prompt text a universal sandbox and does not authorize publication. Repository
protection and distinct promotion identity remain under `PROTECT-001`; immutable publication and
rollback remain under `RELEASE-001`. Retiring the Codex/Sol Actions broker does not reopen the closed
local/external role split or typed evidence contract.
